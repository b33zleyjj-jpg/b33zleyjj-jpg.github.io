Email b33zley.jj@gmail.com. For a bug, it helps to include your device model, iOS version, and what you were doing when it happened.

About the game

A set of balancing beams hangs from the ceiling. Each turn you hang a weighted charm on a hook. Keep every beam balanced, pop three of a colour on one beam for big points, and don't let anything tip past its limit, or it all comes crashing down.

Common questions

How do I play?

Your first game is a short guided tutorial. You can read the rules any time in the game: tap the gear icon, then How to play, or replay the tutorial from the same screen.

Why don't I see the extra life offer?

The extra life is unlocked by watching a short video ad, so it only appears when an ad is ready to play. If you're offline or no ad is available, the game still works normally, just without the extra life.

Can I change my ad and privacy choices?

Yes. To stop apps tracking you, go to iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. If you're in a region where the game asks for ad consent, you can change it in the game: gear icon → Privacy choices. See the privacy policy for details.

How do I reset my best score?

Your scores are stored only on your device. Deleting and reinstalling the app starts you fresh.

Is there a way to remove ads?

Not at the moment. The game is free and paid for by ads.

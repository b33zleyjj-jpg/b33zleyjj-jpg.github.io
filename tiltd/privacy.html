tiltd Privacy Policy

Effective 1 November 2026 · J J Beezley, Australia · tiltd support

The short version
  • You don't need an account, and we never ask for your name, email or any other personal details.
  • The game is free and paid for by ads. To show ads, understand how the game is played and fix crashes, it uses Google AdMob, Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics.
  • Ads are only personalised if you agree, both in our consent screen (where it's required) and in Apple's tracking prompt.
  • Your scores and game history stay on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Who we are

tiltd ("the game", "we", "us") is a game for iPhone and iPad made by J J Beezley. If you have any questions about this policy or your data, contact us at b33zley.jj@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

The game saves a small amount of information on your device so it can work and adjust to you:

  • your best score and how many games you've played;
  • how many games in a row you've finished without beating your best;
  • how many times you've been offered, and have chosen to watch, a rewarded ad;
  • whether you've completed the tutorial.

The game uses this to adjust its difficulty: for example, it eases off after a run of tough games and gives new players a gentler start. This information is not sent to us and is deleted when you delete the app.

What is collected by our service providers

We use three Google services. They collect some information automatically when you play.

Google Analytics for Firebase (how the game is played)

Firebase tells us how people use the game so we can make it better. It collects:

  • Gameplay events we send: when a game starts and ends, your score, how many turns you played, how the game ended, whether you used an extra life, how far through the game's backgrounds you got, how many games you've played and the game's difficulty level at the start, your progress through the tutorial, and when ads are offered or watched.
  • Usage and device information collected automatically: app sessions and how long they last, first launch, app version, device model, operating system version, language, and approximate location (country and region, worked out from your IP address).
  • Identifiers: a random app instance ID created by Firebase, and Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking.

We see this information as statistics. We do not use it to identify you personally.

Firebase Crashlytics (crash reports)

If the game crashes, Crashlytics sends a crash report the next time you open it, so we can find and fix the problem. A report contains technical information only: what the app was doing when it crashed, the app version, device model, operating system version, whether the device was low on memory or storage, and a random identifier for the app installation. It does not include your advertising identifier.

Google AdMob (ads)

AdMob shows the ads that keep the game free: a banner at the bottom of the screen, a full-screen ad between some games, and an optional video ad you can choose to watch for an extra life. To deliver and measure ads, AdMob collects:

  • your IP address, device and operating system information, and approximate location;
  • information about the ads you see and tap;
  • Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking.

If you agree to personalised ads, Google may use this information to show you ads based on your interests. If you don't, you'll still see ads, but they won't be personalised, and Google uses the information only for limited purposes such as counting impressions and preventing fraud.

Google's own policies explain how it uses this information:

Legal bases (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

We rely on your consent for personalised advertising and for storing or reading information on your device for advertising purposes. You give or refuse this in the consent screen shown before any ads load, and you can change it at any time in Settings → Privacy choices. We rely on our legitimate interests for non-personalised ads, analytics that help us fix and improve the game, and fraud prevention.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under the GDPR, the UK GDPR, the Australian Privacy Act, and US state laws such as California's), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information about you, to object to or restrict its use, and to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for targeted advertising. Using the choices above is the quickest way to exercise most of these. For anything else, email b33zley.jj@gmail.com.

Because we don't collect your name or contact details, we usually can't link analytics or ad data to you personally. If you contact us, we'll explain what we can do and help you make your request directly to Google where needed. In Australia, you can also complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner; in the EEA or UK, to your local data protection authority.

How long data is kept

Firebase keeps user-level analytics data for 14 months, after which only aggregated reports remain. Crash reports are kept for 90 days. Google keeps advertising data according to its own policies. Data saved on your device stays until you delete the app.

International transfers

Google may process information in countries other than your own, including the United States. Google uses safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses for these transfers.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age for consent in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has used the game and want their information removed, contact us.

Security

Information sent to Google is encrypted in transit. We don't run our own servers or hold a database of players.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
